## Dataset provenance and scientific decisions

GPM_3IMERGM 07; semantic ID `precipitation`; processed/source field
`precipitation`; Nile rectangle [29,24,33,32]; shape (288,80,40); coverage
2001-01..2024-12; proleptic Gregorian calendar. Source cube hash, fill encoding,
source metadata/guide references and retained QA fields are recorded in
`PHASE_1_INPUT_METADATA.json`.

NASA's [IMERG V07 technical documentation](https://gpm.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-07/IMERG_TechnicalDocumentation_final_230713.pdf)
describes the monthly precipitation value as a rate averaged across the
calendar month. The [NASA data directory](https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/directory)
identifies Final Run rate units as mm/hr. The actual cube also declares mm/hr
and a monthly mean rate. Therefore integrating by calendar-month hours gives
monthly mm; it does not turn the rate directly into an annual total. Tests
cover 28/29-day February and 30/31-day months.

The bundled V07 ATBD was inspected for product context but did not provide a
direct mm/hr conversion statement in the extracted sections; no claim is made
that it did. The technical-document and directory references above provide
the relevant support. No new PDF or source cube was authored or modified.

GPM `precipitationQualityIndex` and `randomError` remain intact. No arbitrary
quality threshold was introduced; these fields are not silently treated as a
validated screening policy. Full QA choices and coverage eligibility are the
next phase. No regional trend, interval, p-value, FDR or significance output
was generated by this checkpoint.
